## Required before publishing

1. Install dependencies and run:
   - `pnpm check`
   - `pnpm build`
2. Review the production output on desktop and mobile.
3. Kit opt-in form and five-email sequence connected: `https://allein.kit.com/b41a314882`.
4. Tally coaching application connected: `https://tally.so/r/dWeQJz`.
5. The final Kit email links to the published Tally application.
6. Confirm the coaching support and privacy inboxes are monitored.
7. Have the Privacy Policy, Website Terms, and separate coaching agreement reviewed for the final operating model.
8. Commit approved source changes to `main`.
9. Publish the verified `dist` output to `gh-pages` while preserving `CNAME`.
10. Verify `https://alleinfitness.com`, `/#/privacy`, and `/#/terms` after deployment.

## Do not add before the first launch

- A large multi-product catalog.
- App download claims before the app is actually available.
- Testimonials, client counts, or outcome claims that cannot be substantiated.
- Complex analytics or advertising trackers before the privacy disclosures and consent approach are updated.
- Extra pages that distract from the Self-Check and Founding Coaching application.
